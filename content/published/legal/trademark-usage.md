# Trademark Usage Guidelines

By using OpenOrgOS brand materials, you agree to the [Terms of Use](/legal/terms), these Trademark Usage Guidelines, and our [Brand Guidelines](/about/brand).

## Ownership

OpenOrgOS, OpenOrgOS Community, and related logos are community marks. Goodwill from their use accrues to the OpenOrgOS Community ecosystem. We may review use at any time and request changes or cessation of use.

## Permitted uses

- **Factual reference** — Describing interoperability, modules, or participation when accurate.
- **Community roles** — Stating verified roles (Maintainer, Committee member, certified holder) with links to public registry pages.
- **Linking** — Linking to openorgos.org or community pages using plain text or approved logos per brand guidelines.

## Restrictions

- Do not use our marks in a way that suggests **endorsement, partnership, or certification** unless explicitly granted.
- Do not incorporate our marks into your logo, product name, or domain name.
- Do not use modified or low-quality versions of our logos.
- Do not use marks in a misleading, defamatory, or unlawful context.

## Certification and modules

Reference certifications (OOO, OOD) only with a valid certificate number and link to the [verification page](/certifications). Reference modules only with their official registry slug and lifecycle status.

## Reporting misuse

Report confusing or unauthorized trademark use via [Contact Us](/about/contact) or GitHub Issues.

## Updates

These guidelines may be updated from time to time. Continued use after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised terms.
