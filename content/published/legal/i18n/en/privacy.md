# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-09

This Privacy Policy describes how **Southwood Inc.** (“we”, “us”) handles personal information on **OpenOrgOS Community** (https://community.oorgos.org, the “Service”).

The Service is the open-source community website for the **OpenOrgOS** organizational communication protocol. We comply with applicable privacy laws, including Japan’s Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI).

---

## 1. Information we collect

### (1) Information you provide

- Community profile (display name, specialty, region, bio, etc.)
- Module role applications and committee participation forms
- Contact and inquiry messages

### (2) Information from OAuth providers

When you authorize a connection, we may receive:

| Service | Purpose | Examples |
|---------|---------|----------|
| Google | Primary OpenOrgOS login | Email, name, profile image |
| GitHub | Developer identity (after sign-in) | User ID, login, email, avatar |
| LinkedIn | Professional identity (Connect only) | Member ID, name, headline, organization |

We do **not** offer LinkedIn-only sign-in. Link GitHub or LinkedIn after Google sign-in from Connections.

### (3) Automatically collected information

- IP address, browser type, OS, access logs
- Cookies and similar technologies for session maintenance
- Usage data (pages viewed, language preference)

### (4) What we do not collect

- **Tenant business data** from your organization — we do not host company operational data
- Unnecessary social posts or private messages from LinkedIn or GitHub

---

## 2. How we use information

We use collected information to:

- Provide authentication, profiles, and community participation features
- Operate module governance, committees, and certification workflows
- Improve the Service and respond to inquiries
- Protect against abuse and enforce our terms

---

## 3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share data with:

- Infrastructure providers (hosting, email) under contract
- OAuth providers as required for authentication you initiate
- Authorities when required by law

Public profile fields you choose to complete may be visible to other members.

---

## 4. Retention and security

We retain information while your account is active and as required by law. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards; no method is 100% secure.
