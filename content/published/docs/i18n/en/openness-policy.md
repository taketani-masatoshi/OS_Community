# Openness Scope & Enterprise Asset Protection

## Core philosophy

OpenOrgOS does not aim to take away an organization's competitive advantage.

We provide **the common foundation organizations need to function** — and standardize non-competitive domains so each company can focus on what differentiates it.

## Domains we aim to standardize

For organizational operations shared across many companies, we encourage modularizing and returning them to the community where possible.

- Accounting
- HR
- Time & attendance
- Contract management
- IP management
- Workflow
- Approval chains
- Governance
- Internal controls
- ISO compliance
- ISMS compliance
- Audit readiness
- Grant management
- Board operations
- Shareholder relations

These are shared infrastructure for business activity — not sources of competitive advantage.

## Domains we do not aim to standardize

Assets that define company-specific competitive advantage should stay separate from OpenOrgOS and be managed by each company.

- Product design
- Drawings
- CAD data
- AI models
- Training data
- Customer data
- Manufacturing know-how
- Sales know-how
- Research data
- Proprietary algorithms
- Patent strategy
- Business strategy
- Proprietary business processes

These are essential assets that make a company what it is. OpenOrgOS never requires sharing or publishing them.

## Private modules

Modules built on OpenOrgOS do not have to be public.

Companies may freely combine **Community Modules (public)** and **Private Modules (internal)**.

OpenOrgOS welcomes returning good modules to the community but does not require it.

## Fundamental principle

OpenOrgOS does not aim to share competitive domains.

We aim for **a world where non-competitive domains are shared so each company can focus on its true competitive edge**.

## Message

Adopting OpenOrgOS does not make every company the same.

Only the organizational foundation is commonized. What defines organizational value — **technology, products, customers, know-how, brand, and strategy** — remains protected as each company's assets.

OpenOrgOS does not strip away what makes your company unique. It is the foundation that lets you concentrate on the domains where uniqueness matters.

---

**OpenOrgOS is not an OS to standardize competitive domains.**

**OpenOrgOS is an OS to standardize non-competitive domains and focus management resources on competitive ones.**
